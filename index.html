Sonali Srivastava

PhD Candidate, Virginia Tech

I am a Ph.D. student in Environmental Engineering at Virginia Tech, specializing in biosensing and AI-driven analytics, and concurrently pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science. My work lives at the intersection of biosensing, molecular biology, and AI-driven data science, combining experimental approaches with computational methods to develop innovative solutions for global health. I’m interested in bringing these two worlds together to develop innovative solutions for global health.

About Me

Experience

Formulation Development Intern, Regeneron Pharmaceuticals (May 2025 – Aug 2025)

  • Developed ML-based chemometric models achieving >95% accuracy for predicting excipients and protein concentrations in formulated drug substances within 5 minutes.

Graduate Research Assistant, Virginia Tech, Vikesland Lab (Aug 2022 – Present)

  • Synthesized, functionalized, and characterized gold–silver core–shell nanoparticles for surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS)-based biosensing applications.
  • Designed and developed nanoparticle-based biosensing platforms integrating electrokinetic enrichment for sensitive and rapid detection of viruses.
  • Integrated and analyzed multimodal analytical datasets, including LC-MS/MS proteomics and SERS, to quantitatively characterize molecular changes and biological responses to nanoparticle exposure and viral infection.

Graduate Research Assistant, Virginia Tech, Sashittal Lab (Dec 2025 – Present)

  • Developed a machine learning model (GM-VAE) to identify different cell states from high-dimensional single-cell RNA sequencing data.
  • Built Python-based computational pipelines for single-cell data preprocessing, representation learning, and model evaluation using HPC infrastructure and SLURM-managed clusters.

Graduate Research Assistant, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (Aug 2020 – Aug 2022)

  • Analyzed multimodal spectroscopy and microscopy datasets (Raman, XRD, SEM-EDS) to characterize chemical transformations for carbon sequestration in cement.

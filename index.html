Sonali Srivastava

PhD Candidate, Virginia Tech

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Virginia Tech in Environmental Engineering, specializing in biosensing and AI-driven analytics. I am concurrently pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, with a focus on computational biology and machine learning applications. I hold an M.S. in Construction Materials from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research integrates wet lab techniques — including nanoparticle synthesis, SERS-based immunoassays, and spectroscopy — with dry lab methods such as machine learning and data analytics, to develop innovative solutions for global health.

About Me

Experience

Formulation Development Intern, Regeneron Pharmaceuticals (May 2025 – Aug 2025)

  • Developed ML-based chemometric models achieving >95% accuracy for predicting excipients and protein concentrations in formulated drug substances within 5 minutes.

Graduate Research Assistant, Virginia Tech (Aug 2022 – Present)

  • Developing a Gaussian Mixture Variational Autoencoder (GM-VAE) to model cellular heterogeneity and infer continuous perturbation trajectories for single-cell sequencing data.
  • Investigated protein expression changes in mammalian cells upon nanoparticle and influenza virus exposure using proteomics and Surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy.
  • Developed ultrasensitive SERS-based biosensors for pathogen detection by integrating electrokinetic enrichment modules to enhance analyte capture and sensitivity.
  • Engineered metal nanoparticles using sol-gel synthesis and performed advanced characterization.

Graduate Research Assistant, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (Aug 2020 – Aug 2022)

  • Conducted a carbonation study on cementitious materials for CO2 sequestration.
  • Characterized carbonated phases using Raman spectroscopy, SEM, and XRD.
